Eigenframe Studio

Eigenframe Studio is a private tool that prepares and uploads videos to two YouTube channels owned by its operator: Eigenframe (mathematics and physics explainers) and TinkerGob (character comedy).

It signs in with Google only to upload videos the operator has approved and to read basic channel information (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly). It is not offered to other users.
